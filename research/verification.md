## What is wrong, and what it should be

1. **fibres.silk.mulberry: 63 (57-68) is not supported.** The source's highest figure is 64.9 USD/kg (Q1 2024), and 2025 runs about 57.3-60.5. Suggested fix: `usd_per_kg` about 59.5, range [57, 61] (2025 quarterly averages plus Sep 2025). If 2024 is included, use [57, 65] with midpoint about 61. Either way, take 68 out.
2. **Polène teardown "~130-180":** the source gives a single total of **$180** (leather $50 + materials $30 + labour $100). Use $180 only. The bank's derived numbers do not appear to use 130, so this is a claim fix, not a math fix.
3. **landed.duty.US Vietnam 12.5%: the number is right, but the cited source (ey_301_2026, EY 2026-1603) never mentions Vietnam.** Add or replace the source with `https://taxnews.ey.com/news/2026-1607-ustr-finalizes-section-301-forced-labor-tariffs-on-60-economies-additional-tariffs-of-10-percent-or-125-percent-take-effect-24-july-2026`. That page lists Vietnam (and China and Türkiye) at 12.5%, and says the EU's combined rate is 10%. Side finding: the bank's `add` map has no entries for **china** or **turkey**, which the same action puts at 12.5%, so US-bound goods from those countries currently get no add-on.
4. **Mansur Gavriel $66:** correct, but its page (`https://tannerleatherstein.com/post/mansur-gavriel`) has no entry in `sources`. Add one.

## Smaller wording fixes (numbers OK)

- Linen `how`: "€3.31 to €9.00 over 2024-25" should say "€3.31 (Jan 2025) to €9.00 (Mar 2024 peak)". The 2024-25 campaign's high was €8.69.
- Woven conversion `how`: $2.81 / 0.29 kg works out to about $9.63/kg, not $9.70. The 1.6 m / 1.52 m / 120 g/m² assumptions are Filo's own, not CBI's. Mark them as judgement.
- Cashmere: the numbers are WFS's micron benchmarks for natural yarn. WFS's own grade table puts Commercial at $60-100 and Luxury at $180-300, so "commercial 90-120" sits at the top of what WFS calls commercial.
