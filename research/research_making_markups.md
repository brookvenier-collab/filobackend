### 3a. Ecobalyse (French government textile method): economic inputs

Source files, current master branch:
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MTES-MCT/ecobalyse/master/src/Data/Textile/Economics.elm
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MTES-MCT/ecobalyse/master/public/data/textile/products.json
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MTES-MCT/ecobalyse/master/src/Data/Unit.elm

Confidence is H for all of these, since they are the method code itself.

**Method rules:**

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Non-physical durability coefficient range | 0.67 (min) to 1.45 (max) |
| Composition of the coefficient | 50% number-of-references index + 50% repair-cost index, scaled linearly between the bounds |
| References index | 100% at ≤1,000 references; falls linearly to 50% at 7,000 and to 0% at 16,000; 0% above 16,000 |
| Repair-cost index | 1 when the repair-cost/price ratio is below 0.33; 0 when it is above 1; interpolated in between |
| Repair-cost index by business type | Large firm with repair service: 0.67 × index + 0.33. Large firm without service: 0.67 × index. SME/micro: the index alone |
| Price field | Abstract (no currency); allowed range 1–1,000 |

**Default price and repair cost by product** (all defaults use a large firm without repair service and 100,000 references):

| Product | Default price | Default repair cost |
|---|---|---|
| T-shirt/polo | 10 | 10 |
| Shirt | 15 | 10 |
| Skirt/dress | 15 | 19 |
| Jeans | 20 | 14 |
| Trousers/shorts | 20 | 14 |
| Sweater | 20 | 15 |
| Coat/jacket | 40 | 31 |
| Socks | 4 | 9 |
| Underwear | 4 | 9 |
| Swimsuit | 15 | 9 |

**Making countries in the Ecobalyse textile list** (https://raw.githubusercontent.com/MTES-MCT/ecobalyse/master/public/data/countries.json, H):
- Countries: Bangladesh, China, France, India, Cambodia, Morocco, Myanmar, Pakistan, Tunisia, Turkey, Vietnam.
- Regions: Western Europe, Eastern Europe, Asia, Africa, Middle East, Latin America, North America, Oceania.
- Default: "unknown country", which is treated as Asia with the worst aquatic-pollution scenario.
- Portugal, Italy and Romania are not listed individually; they fall under the regional entries.
- Aquatic-pollution scenario: Best = France and both Europe regions. Average = China, Cambodia, Morocco, Tunisia, Turkey, Vietnam. Worst = Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, Myanmar, Asia.

A secondary blog describes the same formula and gives category repair costs that match the code: https://projetcelsius.com/blog/ecobalyse-outil-affichage-environnemental-textile/ (M; updated 2026-10-18).

How to read the fibre coefficients: going from 100% polyester to 100% wool shifts the log price by about 100 × 0.012 = 1.2, which multiplies price by about 3.3 for women's outerwear **(derived)**. These coefficients mix fibre cost with positioning, so treat them as market price premiums, not making cost.

## Gaps and caveats

1. **SMV coverage is thin.** I have reliable minutes only for shirts (about 16–31) and trousers or jeans (about 12–34). There are no fetched SMVs for a knit sweater, dress, or tailored coat/jacket; Ecobalyse's 60/120-minute complexity tiers are a method proxy only. A published standard-minute table, such as the GSD/Methods Workshop "SAM ranges by product" image on onlineclothingstudy (not text-readable), would fill this.
2. **No direct CMT price quotes per country for Turkey, Portugal, Italy, Romania, France or Canada/US.** I only have wages. CMT for these countries has to be modelled as wage × SMV × burden factor; the CBI example implies about 1.6x burden plus about 20% profit.
3. **Wage data are mixed.** Some figures are minimum wages and some are averages; years range from 2020 to 2026, and India and Turkey have no garment-specific figures. Indian state minimum wages (Karnataka, Tamil Nadu) could not be fetched. Italy has no statutory minimum, and no apparel-sector wage was fetched for Italy or Romania. Current Eurostat hourly labour costs for NACE C14 were not retrieved; the 2007 BLS series is the latest comparable one.
4. **Markups by model are weak for DTC, premium and luxury.** These figures are company claims or unverified commenter estimates. The planned brand transparency pages (Asket, Sézane, Patagonia, Everlane product-level "true cost") could not be fetched: the Asket page errored and the search budget ran out. No McKinsey or BoF figure for cost-to-retail multiples was found. No luxury gross margins (LVMH, Kering, Hermès) were retrieved; only Hermès' operating margin.
5. **US tariff stack is volatile.** IEEPA ended in February 2026, Section 122 expired in July 2026, and Section 301 add-ons of 10–12.5% apply from 2026-07-24, with textile TRQs and exemptions whose apparel details were not fetched. China's current total stack (MFN + 7.5% legacy 301 + any new 301) is unconfirmed. Re-verify before shipping.
6. **Canada woven (ch. 62) lines** and the current-year CBSA schedule were not fetched line by line. The 18% MFN is confirmed for knit only, from the 2024 archived page plus a 2026 aggregator for one line.
7. **No item-level average retail prices for North America.** Statistics Canada and BLS give spending and CPI indices, not average prices; Statista and Circana values are paywalled. There is no North American equivalent of Refashion's 15.6 EUR per item. Per-garment spend can only be derived if a units figure is found (for example, AAFA ApparelStats garments per capita, which was not fetched).
8. **France vs North America spend per garment:** France is 15.6 EUR per item (2024, Refashion) and the UK is 16.70 GBP (2023, WRAP). No comparable fetched figure exists for Canada or the US.
9. **IFM consumer-panel average price per piece** was not found in public pages; only the price change of about +1% in 2024. JRC (EU) data were not reached.
10. The web search budget for this session was exhausted before items 4, 7 and 9 could be pursued further. A follow-up session could continue.
